On the click, the whole page, header included, shrinks and darkens on a dark background, and a sheet the colour of the page slides up over it. Once the next page is ready, the sheet fades and the new content comes in. Going back looks the same: another card on the pile.

The page that steps back is a still copy kept in the layer, so the real page never moves. Videos and canvases are copied as they are on screen, and components and embeds don't start again.

Inspired by the project page transition on [Olga Prudka's site](https://olgaprudka.com/).
