On the click, five bars rise from the bottom, each a beat behind the last (three on phones). They stay while the next page loads, then carry on up in the same order, so the screen clears like a row of blinds, and the new content comes in. Going back, they start from the right.

Each bar is a panel as tall as the screen, starting a little after the one before.
